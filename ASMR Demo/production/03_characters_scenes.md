# Characters, scene & reference prompts

**Approved design: `../art/Character.jpg`.** It holds turnarounds, eight expressions each, six action poses each (ear-whisper, side-to-side, run, cold/warm breath, spell, rest/hug) and prop details. Use it as the image reference for every shot. The tables below summarise it, and the browser animatic follows it. The prompts are only for regenerating extra sheets in the same design.

## Consistency rules
- Same hair, ornament side and ear/tail colours in every shot: Koyuki's flower ornament is on her right side; Homura has a ribbon and bell on **both** sides.
- Kimono overlap left-over-right (correct for the living).
- Koyuki = cool blues and white light. Homura = orange/gold light. The room is warm from the hearth and blue from the window.
- Close-ups at the ear: face partly outside the frame, eyes looking toward the viewer, lips near the lens edge on that side.
