# Characters, scene & reference prompts

Put the existing approved designs (turnarounds / four-views / full-body art) in `production/characters/` and use them as image references for every shot. The prompts below regenerate missing sheets **in the same design**. They are not meant to replace approved art.

## Consistency rules
- Same hair length, bangs shape, hairpin side, ear/tail colours in every shot. Koyuki's pin is always on her left, Homura's bell on her left.
- Kimono overlap left-over-right (correct for the living).
- Koyuki = cool blues and white light. Homura = orange/gold light. The room is warm from the hearth and blue from the window.
- Close-ups at the ear: face partly outside the frame, eyes looking toward the viewer, lips near the lens edge on that side.

## Reference prompts

**Turnaround (each character):**
> Character turnaround sheet, front / three-quarter / side / back views, full body, neutral pose, flat light grey background, original Japanese 2D anime style, clean line art, cel shading. [Koyuki or Homura block from 02_storyboard_seedance.md]

**Expression sheet:**
> Expression sheet, 9 head-and-shoulders panels: smile, shy blush, closed-eye happy laugh, smug grin, whispering with half-closed eyes, pursed lips blowing, pout with puffed cheeks, surprised, sleepy. Original Japanese 2D anime, consistent design. [character block]

**Background plate (empty room, POV):**
> First-person view seated on tatami inside a small traditional Japanese cabin at night, empty: open shoji window in the centre showing snowy pine hills, falling snow and a full moon; lit paper shoji panels on both sides; dark wooden beams and posts; hanging scroll on the left; andon lantern on the right; irori hearth at the bottom edge glowing orange. Original Japanese 2D anime background art, soft painterly lighting, 16:9.

**FX plates (optional, for compositing):** frost-breath mist stream on black · golden warm breath with embers on black · foxfire wisp loop on black · snowflake + foxfire spiral vortex on black.
