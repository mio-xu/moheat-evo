## Story (5 steps ≈ 2:40 + 3 short free-talk windows; then 「もっと話す」)
1. **吹雪の峠** — white-out, gusts sweep left → right · Nagisa finds you · 「助けて…」/「あなたは…？」 · action 手をとる · her fur cape.
2. **宿・火鍋** — Hinata's hotpot (steam in front) + brazier (right) · bet 「大！」/「小！」 (say it) · soup bowl at your face · she
   whispers at your right ear that she cheated · **free-talk window ①** (Hinata).
3. **女医の手当て** — Mint: **moxa circling your right ear (moving warmth) + mint balm on the same ear (cool)**, hold 「じっとする」 (let go
   = again), her breath on the balm (heat → cool in ~1 s), eyes closed → whisper at the left ear · **window ②** (Mint, yes/no).
4. **夜番** — the front door bursts open on your LEFT (cold gust), Nagisa stands in the wind there (cold drops, her shoulder warm) ·
   **window ③** (Nagisa, yes/no) · say 「おつかれさま」/「ありがとう」 · whisper at the left ear.
5. **夜明けの峠** — all three at dawn · take one token (3 endings: star hairpin / dice / orange hair tie) → her last whisper at your ear →
   you wake up in the present → this demo's cover with the ending text.

**Free talk** (shared engine `demos/shared/talk.js`): each window = one turn of yours (voice in the display language or typing,
10 s silence = skip) → a bridge line chosen by topic (`lines.js` `topics`: leave / thanks / future / game / food / sleep / cold /
praise / name, yes / no after a yes-no question, else generic / silence; never twice) → the story goes on. **もっと話す · Keep talking**
appears on the cover after the story: Mint (her room) · Hinata (the hotpot table) · Nagisa (the night door) · all three (dawn) —
scripted beats + bridges here; the **live AI** version of the same choices + the same windows is the private `live/index.html`
(generated by `scripts/pages.py`; `live/live.js` = prompt + parser on `MoHeatTalk.live`) served only by `tools/talk/server.py`
(`/live/inn`, password `~/.moheat_live_pass`, limits shared with Demo23, voices from `tools/talk/demos.json`).

## Thermal (firmware DUAL)
Voices warm the ear they speak at: `(0.1 + 0.8 × level)` × distance (ear 1 / ≤ 50 cm 0.6 / further 0.28), × 0.5 in front.
Ambient: blizzard (both cool 0.3) + gusts sweeping left → right (moving cool 0.45, cut by the fur cape) · hotpot steam (front warm
0.3) · brazier (right warm 0.18) · soup bowl (front warm 0.45) · moxa circling the right ear (moving warm 0.55) + mint balm on the
same ear (right cool 0.45) + her breath on it (right cool 0.85) · paper lamp (left 0.1) · front door open on the left (left cool 0.65,
0.15 with Nagisa in front, her shoulder left warm 0.18) · dawn sun (front warm 0.15) + snow (cool 0.12). Not heated: the bet, blushes.

## Files / pipeline
- One source for every line: `scripts/script_data.py` (tagged ja + en + zh) → `lines.js`, `project/zh_lines.json`; `scripts/voice.py`
  (ElevenLabs, cache `audio/voice_cache.json`, 105 lines / 3,814 characters, 547 s) → `media/voice/`; `scripts/make_l10n.py` → `l10n.js`.
- Art = the layered standard (guide §6): `scripts/prompts.py` → `project/web_prompts.json` → `python3 tools/openai/images.py <this
  folder> <jobs>` (Seedance API probe 2026-11-08: 429, Retry-After 64,409 s → OpenAI); masked blink variants from
  `project/face_points.json`; **mouths = LivePortrait sets** (`"mouth": "lp"`, `~/ml/LivePortrait/.venv/bin/python tools/art/lp_mouths.py
  <this folder> --sheet`) for every pose except `hinata_ear` (no face found → drawn masked variant + 3 `regions_mouth` along her smile); `scripts/make_plates.py` (dawn = graded pass, free) → `scripts/make_stage.py` → `project/stage.json` →
  `python3 tools/art/build_stage.py <this folder>` → `scripts/despill.py` (Mint's magenta screen) → `scripts/make_trio.py` (the group at dawn + the cover group from the cut-outs, free).
- `?auto=1` / `?auto=1&alt=1` / `&end=0|1|2` test the branches and endings, `?talk=M|H|N|MHN` (with `?auto`) the keep-talking mode,
  `?flat` flat stills, `?cover=1` poster (`media/cover.jpg`).
