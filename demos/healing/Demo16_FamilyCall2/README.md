# じいちゃんの、ゆきだるま · Grandpa's Little Snowman (Part 2)

Part 2 of the call home to grandma Hana and grandpa Gen (Part 1: [`../Demo15_FamilyCall/`](../Demo15_FamilyCall/)). The same call a minute later:
they are on speaker on your table and grandma's scarf is round your neck (both ears warm). Grandpa's refrigerated mini snowman on your right cheek
(right cool, left warm), the phone back to your ear, a New Year promise, and nobody wants to hang up first.

This folder holds only the page and its cover: `index.html` sets `<base href="../Demo15_FamilyCall/">` and `<body data-part="2">`, so the code, art and
voices are Part 1's files (one `main.js` runs either part). Details in Part 1's README.
