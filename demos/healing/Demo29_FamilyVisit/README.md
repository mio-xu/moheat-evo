# ただいま、じいちゃん・ばあちゃん · Home for New Year — MoHeat Evo ASMR demo

Interactive (~2:30): Part 3 of the grandparents series (Demo15 *Hello, Grandma?* and Demo16 *Grandpa's Little Snowman*). This time you go home to Hana and Gen in person. The user picked plan 1 out of 3 and approved the storyboard on 2026-11-08.

## Story
1. **The snowy little station at dusk**: the train pulls out behind you and the wind blows in from the open left side. Grandpa waves, comes up to you, and spots the red scarf Grandma knitted in Part 1.
   - Choice: "I'm home!" / "You came to pick me up?"
2. **The genkan**: the door slides open and the warm air of the house reaches you. Grandma rushes out and hugs you, whispering at your left ear.
   - Say "I'm home" (voice-judged).
3. **The kotatsu**: the kerosene stove is on your right, and Grandma holds out a steaming bowl of oshiruko.
   - Hold to blow on it.
   - Choice: a mandarin (Grandpa peels it) / more oshiruko (Grandma is delighted).
4. **Next morning in the garden**: Grandpa's snowball whizzes past your right ear.
   - Throw one back. Grandma calls from the veranda that the amazake is ready.
5. **Night**: Grandma at your pillow on the left tells how Grandpa proposed on a snowy day, while Grandpa snores next door on the right. She tucks the quilt up to your ears.
   - Choice: "I'll come again" / "I want to stay here forever". Her last whisper differs; Grandpa mumbles in his sleep. Good night.

## Thermal (DUAL, live)
- **Voices**: warm at the ear they speak at, scaled by loudness and distance. Grandpa shouting from 6 m is barely warm; Grandma's whispers at your left ear are the warmest.
- **Ambient**:
  - Outdoor air (cool, both ears) and the station wind from the left (cool, with gusts).
  - The red scarf (mild warmth, both ears).
  - Warm air from the open genkan door (front).
  - Grandma's cheek on your left ear during the hug.
  - The stove on your right.
  - The steaming bowl in front of your face.
  - The snowball past your right ear (a short cold burst).
  - The quilt over both ears (slow warmth).
- **Deliberately not heated**: the kotatsu. It warms your legs, not your ears.

## Assets (stills first, DEMO_DESIGN_GUIDE §6.3)
- **Art**: 5 Seedance GPT Image 2 stills (`scripts/seedance.py`, about 125 credits). The references are Demo15's approved frames (design A, soft cel).
  - Staging (L0): slow drift, push-in toward whoever speaks, cross-fades.
  - Effects (L1): snow, warm doorway light, stove glow, steam, the snowball, andon flicker, the quilt.
- **Voices**: ElevenLabs `hana` r1_c and `gen` r1_a, the user's picks for Demo15/16. Generated by `scripts/voice.py`.
- **Sound**: Web Audio with HRTF (`audio.js`): wind, train, footsteps on snow, sliding door, stove, whoosh, snoring, quilt.
