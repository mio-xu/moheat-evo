## Story (5 parts, ≈ 2:30)
1. **午後の暑さ** — a hot Sunday afternoon in a tatami room (both ears warm .25), cicadas, the glass wind chime. Naomi lies flat as a cat
   pancake. You switch on the oscillating fan: cool air sweeps left ↔ right (4 s period).
2. **飄帯あそび** — the fan's paper streamer flutters; Naomi bats at it. Drag the fan's angle / tap 「首振り」 so the streamer stops where she
   can reach (3 hits).
3. **冰瓶と西瓜** (props B) — drag the towel-wrapped ice-water bottle next to her; she presses her cheek to it. You lean in: the cold bottle in
   front of you, her warm cheek at your right = cool + warm on one side. Watermelon on the plate; the uchiwa (tap 「あおぐ」 = gentle cool puffs).
4. **夕立** — the sky darkens, thunder far off, rain blows in at the LEFT window. Choose 「窓を閉める」 (no wind, muggy) / 「すこし開けておく」
   (cool rain air). Thunder scares Naomi: she squeezes against your left side (left warm). Stroke her (drag) to calm her.
5. **夜** — rain stops, insects, the fan on low. Naomi climbs onto your shoulder, purring at your left ear (warm); the fan's cool sweeps
   past on the right. Say 「おやすみ、ナオミ」 (voice-judged) → fade → cover. Also: say 「ナオミ」 anytime → she looks back 「にゃ？」.
