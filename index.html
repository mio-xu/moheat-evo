Feel sound through temperature.

Next-generation thermal headphones. Warmth, coolness and physical presence for voices, characters and immersive media.

  • Warm + coolLED heating and alcohol-mist cooling in one headset
  • Left / right independentEach ear gets its own temperature, just like stereo sound
  • Synced to soundFeedback follows voices, music, films and games in real time
THE MISSING PHYSICAL CUE

The presence behind the voice.

When someone comes close, you feel their warmth. When they breathe softly, a cool touch. MoHeat Evo brings those missing cues to audio.
